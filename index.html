FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work in fullscreen?

Crosshair Studio works in borderless and windowed modes. Most modern games default to borderless and are visually indistinguishable from fullscreen. Exclusive fullscreen blocks all overlays at the OS level.

Will it impact my framerate?

No. The overlay is event-driven and idles at true 0% CPU. The control panel only repaints when animations are running, then sleeps. Rendering uses native Direct2D — no Electron, no web view, no background workers.

Is it really free?

Yes — completely free, with no ads, no tracking, and no premium tier.

Can I make my own crosshair?

Absolutely — that's what the Designer is for. Adjust lines, center dot, gap, thickness, outline, colour, opacity, and rotation. Save unlimited custom crosshairs and switch between them with a hotkey.
